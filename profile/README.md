## Observatory for Technological Questions in Society

In the [Observatory for Technological Questions in Society](https://github.com/echo-observatory), we leverage LLMs and other forms of NLP to parse and map public debates about technology. The ability to continuously semi-automatically monitor how issues emerge, how actors raise alerts, how different types of critique are mobilized, and how controversies enter different phases, allow us to focus our guided qualitative deep dives on significant moments of change and contestation.

## Perspectival Modelling and Deep Digital Twins

In the working group for Perspectival Modelling and Deep Digital Twins, we explore the value biases of LLMs and their potentials for modelling personas and generating synthetic interlocutors based on qualitative insights. We experiment with ways of steering and benchmarking model values, integrating practices like participatory reinforcement learning with ethnographic fieldwork, and ultimately bring deep qualitative insights into agent-based modelling and other forms of simulation.

## Gamification, Elicitation and Public Engagement

In the working group for Gamification, Elicitation and Public Engagement, we use data physicalization and game design to bring our analyses into conversation with relevant stakeholders, elicit feedback, organize deliberation, and find new ways to do public engagement around emergent technological problems in society.
